The **J.I.L.L. Program** (Justice-Impacted Living & Learning) is a six-week, community-based reentry and growth series for justice-impacted people who are ready to stabilize, rebuild, and thrive. Each week blends mental-health support, practical life skills, and resources you can actually use. Groups are small, and everyone who finishes receives a completion certificate.

**Thursdays, 5:15–7:30 PM**, for six consecutive weeks, in person at Random Acts of Kindness, 1010 Antietam, Detroit, MI 48207.

The series rotates through three areas:

- **Mental Therapy & Guidance Counseling** — emotional healing and coping strategies for the pressure of coming home.
- **Digital Power: AI-Enhanced Reentry** — the technology literacy that everyday life now assumes, and how to navigate resources online.
- **Budgeting, Finances & Credit Building** — financial education and rebuilding credit from where you actually stand.

The program is administered by our partner [RAKS (Random Acts of Kindness)](https://raks1.org), a 501(c)(3) serving justice-impacted people. Call [313-444-9671](tel:313-444-9671) to ask about the next cohort.
