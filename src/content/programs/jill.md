The **J.I.L.L. Program** (Justice-Impacted Living & Learning) is a six-week community-based reentry initiative combining mental health support, life skills coaching, and an AI-enhanced digital toolkit — designed to help returning citizens stabilize, rebuild, and thrive.

The program is administered by our partner [RAKS (Random Acts of Kindness)](https://raks1.org), a 501(c)(3) serving justice-impacted people.
