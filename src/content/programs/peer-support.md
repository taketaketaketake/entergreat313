title: Weekly Support Group Meetings
summary: A safe, supportive space for justice-impacted individuals to connect and grow — every Tuesday evening.
order: 1
---

Every Tuesday, 6:00–7:30 PM at 1010 Antietam, Detroit.

A safe place for healing, hope, and connection. No referral needed, no paperwork, no cost — just show up. Anyone seeking support is welcome, and so is anyone ready to give it.

What happens in the room:

- **Judgment-free discussion** — members talk openly about reentry struggles and day-to-day life, and are heard without being managed.
- **Guest speakers** — local leaders, counselors, and mentors bring practical tools for personal growth.
- **Peer support** — trust and accountability built through shared stories from people who have made the same walk.
- **Wellness and emotional well-being** — mindfulness, coping strategies, and confidence-building.
- **Practical guidance** — direct connections to community partners for employment, housing, legal help, and social services.
